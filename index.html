↗ Open Official Taiwan Arrival Card (Free)

📋 What to Prepare Before Filling Out the Form

  1. Passport: valid passport and its personal details (name, passport number, nationality, date of birth, expiration date).
  2. Email: an accessible email address to receive and enter a verification code and receive confirmation.
  3. Contact details: occupation and phone number.
  4. Flight information: arrival date, flight number and travel details from your e-ticket.
  5. Accommodation in Taiwan: hotel name and location (see below).
  6. Return flight: keep your confirmed return itinerary handy if requested.
After submission, check your email for confirmation. Immigration may retrieve your TWAC information using your passport. TWAC is an arrival registration form, not a visa.
