🚆 Airport MRT · Taoyuan Airport to Palais de Chine Hotel

Step 1
Follow signs for Airport MRT
From Terminal 1, use Airport MRT A12; from Terminal 2, use A13. Follow signs for Taoyuan Airport MRT.
Step 2
Take the train to Taipei Main Station (A1)
Follow the purple line for the Express Train toward Taipei Main Station (A1). Fare: NT$160 per person. Travel time is approximately 35–40 minutes; allow extra time for waiting and walking.
Step 3
Walk to Palais de Chine Hotel (君品酒店)
Follow the indoor route below from the station to the hotel. Allow extra time for walking with luggage.

You can also follow the signs for Exit Y5 at Taipei Main Station, then continue toward Q Square / Taipei Bus Station and Palais de Chine Hotel (Chengde Road entrance).

Exit Y5 is an alternative to the indoor route above. Follow station signs and check elevator access if carrying large luggage.
Follow the posted signs for Q Square and the hotel; station access and opening hours may affect the route.
Use public transportation for eligible reimbursement. Taxi / Uber fares are not covered by TTSTC Taiwan. Keep transport receipts or tickets where available. Airport MRT operates on a schedule; check service times for early arrivals.
